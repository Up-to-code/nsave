1. Use of Cookies

A "cookie" is a set of data (a unique identifier) that a server sends to your browser and is stored on your computer. Your browser then provides this unique identifier to be used each time you submit a request to the Website. We use cookies on the Website to, among other things, keep track of the services you have used, record registration information, store your user preferences, keep you logged in to the Website, facilitate your use of the Website, and track the pages you visit. Cookies help us understand how the Website is used and improve your user experience.

2. Our cookies

Our cookies are "first party cookies" (set by our domain; as opposed to third-party cookies), and may be permanent or temporary. These are necessary cookies, without which the Website would not function properly or be able to provide certain features and functionalities. Some of them can be manually disabled in your browser, but may affect the functionality of the Site.
